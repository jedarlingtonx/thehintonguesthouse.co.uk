Welcome to The Hinton

Built in the early 1900s, looked after with real care.

The Hinton was built for a local dignitary and stands in its own grounds in the village of Mobberley, with parking at the front and a good-sized garden at the back.

Our guests tell us it is spotlessly clean, that Ann and the team make them feel welcome, and that the little extras make the difference. Come for a night on the way through, or stay a week and explore.

Comfortable, en-suite rooms

Every room has its own bathroom, a Freeview TV and free Wi-Fi, with housekeeping every day.

Breakfast, your way

Cooked, continental or healthy, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices.

Free parking, on site

Park at the front of the house, with a lock-up for bikes.

A drink in the lounge or garden

We hold an alcohol licence, so you can unwind after a day's work or touring.

Garden and lounge

Somewhere lovely to sit out, or settle in.

Take a drink out to the garden, the terrace or the gazebo. If the weather turns, there is a conservatory, a comfortable lounge and a library of books, DVDs and games.

Breakfast

Pre-ordered, and served in our breakfast room.

Choose a full English or Irish, a continental or a lighter healthy breakfast. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available, and there is a children's menu.
